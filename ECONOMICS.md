## What a Converted Lead Is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. A mid-sized startup buying Cursor Business, Claude Team, and OpenAI API credits might spend $1,500/mo at retail. If Credex offers 25% off retail on a 6-month pre-purchase, the deal value is approximately:

```
$1,500/mo × 6 months × 0.75 (Credex discounted price) = $6,750 revenue to Credex
Credex margin on credits (estimated at 15–20%): $1,012–$1,350 gross profit per deal
```

Conservative estimate: **$1,000 gross profit per converted credit buyer.**

High-spend enterprise customers (spending $5k+/mo on AI) are worth significantly more. Using a conservative blended average of **$1,000 GP per converted lead** for this analysis.

## Customer Acquisition Cost by Channel

| Channel                         | Estimated CAC      | Basis                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| HN/Reddit organic               | ~$0 direct cost    | 0 paid spend; ~5 hrs time. If Credex values time at $100/hr, CAC = $500 time-cost to acquire first cohort of 100 audits, converting 5% → ~$1,000/lead |
| Newsletter placement            | ~$200–400/lead     | Typical dev newsletter sponsorship $500–2000; expect 5–10 leads from a single placement                                                               |

**Blended CAC at early stage: ~$150–300 per converted lead.** GP of $1,000 → CAC payback on first deal. Strong unit economics.

## Conversion Funnel Math

```
Audit completed:         100%  (baseline)
Email captured:           20%  (value-first gate — conservative for B2B)
Consultation booked:       5%  (of email captures = 1% of audits)
Credit purchase closed:   30%  (of consultations booked)

Net: 1 credit buyer per 333 audits completed
Average GP per buyer: $1,000
Revenue per audit completed: $1,000 / 333 = ~$3.00
```

To cover $10k/mo in Credex marketing spend at $3/audit: need **3,333 audits/mo** — very achievable once the HN/newsletter flywheel is running.

## What Would Have to Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

**$1M ARR = $83k gross profit/month = 83 credit deals closed/month.**

Working backward from the funnel:

- 83 deals/mo at 30% close rate from consultations = **277 consultations/mo**
- 277 consultations at 5% of email captures = **5,540 email captures/mo**
- 5,540 captures at 20% conversion from audits = **27,700 audits/mo**

Is 27,700 audits/month achievable in 18 months?

**Month 1–3:** HN launch + newsletter placements → 2,000–5,000 audits/mo. Expensive in time.

**Month 4–9:** SEO starts ranking for "AI spend audit", "cursor vs copilot cost calculator", "claude team pricing" → organic inbound grows to 5,000–12,000 audits/mo.

**Month 9–18:** Partner embeds (AI tool comparison sites, startup toolkits, Credex partner pages) → 15,000–30,000 audits/mo.

**Key assumptions that must hold:**

1. Credex's discount is genuinely attractive (25%+ below retail) — if it shrinks, conversion from consultation to purchase drops.
2. AI tool pricing stays fragmented and opaque enough that the audit remains useful. If vendors start publishing clearer comparison tools themselves, the moat erodes.
3. The sales team can close 30% of consultations. This requires tight qualification (only invite high-savings cases) and a frictionless booking flow.

**Rough model at 18 months:**

| Metric                    | Value    |
| ------------------------- | -------- |
| Monthly audits            | 27,000   |
| Email captures (20%)      | 5,400    |
| Consultations booked (5%) | 270      |
| Deals closed (30%)        | 81       |
| Monthly GP (81 × $1,000)  | $81,000  |
| ARR equivalent            | $972,000 |

Close — achievable with a sales rep and strong top-of-funnel.
