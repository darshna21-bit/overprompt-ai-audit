## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes, completely. No credit card, no login required to run an audit. We ask for your email after showing you the results if you want to save them — never before.

**Q: How do you make money?**
A: For audits that surface significant savings (typically >$500/mo), we connect you with Credex — a platform that sells discounted AI infrastructure credits sourced from companies that overforecast usage. The audit is genuinely useful whether or not you ever talk to Credex.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: The shareable URL strips all identifying information — only tool names and savings numbers are public. Your email is never shown in the shareable link. We don't sell or share your data.

**Q: How current is the pricing data?**
A: Prices are verified against official vendor pricing pages weekly. Every number in the audit engine traces to a URL and verification date in our open-source PRICING_DATA.md. If you spot an error, open a GitHub issue.

**Q: What if the audit says I'm already spending well?**
A: We'll tell you that plainly. "You're spending well" is a real output — we don't manufacture savings. You can sign up to be notified when new optimization opportunities apply to your specific stack.
